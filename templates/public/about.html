About the platform

One farm. Better decisions.

Farm Time Machine helps farmers understand past conditions, explore transparent scenarios, and connect with verified agricultural professionals.

Public first, expertise on demand

Weather and historical exploration stay open to everyone. An account is only needed to save a farm, record decisions, run saved simulations, or request advice.

The platform does not replace a qualified agriculturalist. Forecasts, simulations, and AI responses disclose their source, limits, and uncertainty.

Our principles

  • No login wall for public information
  • Traceable source and freshness labels
  • Actionable plain-language interpretation
  • Backend-enforced role permissions
  • Bangla-ready and accessible patterns
