How can we help?

Read common answers or submit a support ticket. Logged-in users can review ticket status below.

Are sample values real observations?

No. Anything marked Sample or Demonstration exists only to show how the interface works.

Does a simulation predict my harvest?

No. The baseline simulator is deterministic decision support, not a calibrated yield forecast or guarantee.

Who can see my farm?

You control farm-data consent. Agriculturalists can only access information shared for an authorized consultation or assignment.

Why is live weather unavailable?

The provider may be unreachable or live integration may be disabled. The status and last update appear beside the weather card.
