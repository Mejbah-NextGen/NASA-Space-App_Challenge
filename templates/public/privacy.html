Privacy

Privacy by design

How account, farm, consultation, and integration data are handled.

Information you provide

Account details, farm records, support messages, and consultation content are used to provide the requested platform features.

Farm-data sharing

Private farm information is not public. The currently assigned agriculturalist receives selected information only through an authorized assignment and active farmer consent; a credential-review badge does not expand that access.

External providers

Weather and optional AI integrations run through the server. Credentials are never placed in browser code. Only necessary context should be transmitted.

Your choices

You can update profile and notification preferences, revoke farm-data consent, and contact support about account data.

This project policy is a development-ready summary and should receive legal review before a public production launch.
